My Favourite Foods

I enjoy eating a variety of foods, especially those that are tasty and nutritious. Food is important because it gives us energy and keeps us healthy.

Rice

Rice is one of my favorite meals because it is delicious and easy to prepare. I like eating it with stew and chicken. It is also a very popular food in many homes.

Beans

Beans are very healthy because they contain protein. I enjoy eating beans with bread or rice. It is both nutritious and filling.

Fruits

Fruits like apples and bananas are sweet and refreshing. They are important for good health and help the body stay strong.
